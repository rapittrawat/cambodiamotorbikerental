Khmer culture · Khmer hospitality

Ride through living heritage

The Cambodia waiting for you on every CMR ride — pagoda mornings, festival blessings and golden monuments.

Cambodia’s soul is in its gentle sompeah greeting and its world-famous hospitality. During Pchum Ben we hand out sticky rice and candles like in the festival cards above — and before every long journey our team ties a red kromâ scarf band on your mirrors for luck.

  • ☸ Dress covering shoulders & knees when parking at pagodas
  • ☸ Never point your feet at a Buddha image — or at people
  • ☸ Say som toh (sorry) gently, and smile
  • ☸ Ask us where to watch Bonn Om Touk & Khmer New Year

Phnom Penh visiting places

Six spots riders never skip

Every place below is an easy ride from our garage — pick a bike in the morning and see the whole city by sunset.

👑

Royal Palace & Silver Pagoda

Gilded spires and the floor of 5,000 silver tiles — the royal heart of the city, a ten-minute glide down Sothearos Blvd.

🏍️ Riding tip: riverside lanes run one-way — park at the public lot by the palace gate and keep your helmet ticket safe.

Open in Maps ↗
🛕

Wat Phnom

The founding hill of Phnom Penh, shaded by kapok trees and freckled with monkeys — the best sunrise skyline in town.

🏍️ Riding tip: peak-hour traffic on Sihanouk Blvd is dense — approach from the flower-market side and park at the base; walk up, never ride past the shrine.

Open in Maps ↗
🏛️

Central Market

Phsar Thmei's art-deco golden dome — souvenirs, silk, gold and rivers of street-food stalls under one iconic roof.

🏍️ Riding tip: use the motorbike parking under the dome wings, chain-lock your helmet, and keep documents in the trunk — not the basket.

Open in Maps ↗
🗼

Independence Monument

The five-tower lotus icon where Norodom meets Sihanouk Blvd — at night it glows and the surrounding flags fly like a forest.

🏍️ Riding tip: it sits in a five-lane roundabout — keep to the outer lane, signal early and circle once for the perfect photo.

Open in Maps ↗
🧵

Silk Island / Koh Dach

A Mekong island of silk weavers, wooden stilt houses and golden-hour dirt lanes — the classic half-day motorbike escape.

🏍️ Riding tip: take the small ferry at the Chroy Changvar bridge (a few thousand riels per bike) and ride slow — lanes dirt over after rain.

Open in Maps ↗
🌅

Sunset Riverside

Sisowath Quay at golden hour — pale yellow colonial fronts, river wind and grilled snails as the boats drift by.

🏍️ Riding tip: weekend evenings the Koh Pich riverfront closes to cars — the easiest first ride we recommend to new visitors.
